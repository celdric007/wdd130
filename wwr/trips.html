Our Featured River Rafting Trips

Most Popular

South Fork American River

Perfect for families and first-timers seeking true whitewater thrills.

Plunge into the heart of the canyon on this action-packed single-day journey. You will navigate rolling Class III rapids, marvel at towering granite cliffs, and stop mid-day for a gourmet riverside deli lunch prepared fresh by your guide. Keep your eyes peeled for bald eagles and bighorn sheep along the shoreline.

Extreme Adrenaline

Tuolumne River

An intense challenge designed exclusively for experienced paddlers.

Brace yourself for continuous, technical Class IV and V rapids that demand teamwork and precise execution. This high-octane expedition drops through narrow volcanic gorges featuring massive waves, tight drops, and heart-pounding vertical plunges. Wet suits, helmets, and a brief safety certification interview are required before departure.

Scenic & Relaxing

Middle Fork American River

A peaceful escape away from the chaos of everyday life.

If you prefer sweeping mountain vistas over soaking-wet drops, this gentle drift is custom-made for you. Meander down crystal-clear waters winding through lush alpine meadows. This package includes a relaxing swim stop in natural hot springs and plenty of time for photography, making it an excellent choice for children and seniors alike.
